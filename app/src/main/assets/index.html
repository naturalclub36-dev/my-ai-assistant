How to use

1. Tap “Activate Sundri” once.

2. Leave Sundri running and use any app.

3. Say “Sundri”.

4. Then say what you want, e.g. “Open WhatsApp” or “WhatsApp kholo”.

You can also say “Sundri open Instagram” in one sentence.

Sundri needs microphone access and stays active with a visible Android notification. Offline wake/voice works only when the phone's speech service has an offline language model; otherwise it uses the phone's online speech service.
